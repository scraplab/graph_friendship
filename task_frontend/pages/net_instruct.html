Instructions


Please read the instructions carefully before starting the experiment!


This study will consist of 30 trials in which you will make social connections in naturalistic social networks. On each trial, you will be presented with a network graph like the one below. Each circle represents another (imaginary) person, with one red node representing you, the participant. The lines between people represent existing social connections. Your task will be to make a new social connection that maximizes your status in the network, however you define it. You will do so by clicking on or tapping the circle representing the person you wish to make friends with. Each trial is independent of the previous ones - your choices are not cummulative.

Consider each choice carefully. There are no right or wrong answers - we are merely interested in the principles which guide your decisions. If you are ready to start this task, please press the "begin" button below. Do not hit the reload or back buttons during the experiment unless you wish to exit the study.
